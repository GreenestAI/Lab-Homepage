Greenest A.I. Lab will participate in the 2026 Annual Conference of the Korean Society of Environmental Engineers (KSEE), held at Jeju Shinhwa World, Jeju, Republic of Korea, from November 3 to 6, 2026.

A total of seven studies will be presented by members of Greenest A.I. Lab, consisting of six oral presentations and one poster presentation. The presentations cover a wide range of research topics integrating artificial intelligence with environmental and energy systems.

## Oral Presentations

**Prof. SungKu Heo**

- Process-Informed Neural Operator-Based Optimization of Wastewater Treatment Plant Operation
- AI-Based Inverse Engineering for Decentralized Wastewater Treatment
- Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning-Based Data Center Operation Optimization Considering Power and Climate Conditions

**SeonJu Kim**

- AI-Based Autonomous Soft Sensor for N₂O Emission Quantification
- AI-Assisted Mathematical Programming for Integrated Biogasification Facility Design and Supply Chain Optimization

**SangGu Yun**

- Multimodal Transformer-Based Nationwide Spatio-Temporal Particulate Matter Forecasting

## Poster Presentation

**SangGu Yun**

- Development of an LLM-Based Agentic LCA Framework for Autonomous Environmental Assessment


Through these studies, Greenest A.I. Lab presents its recent research on AI-driven wastewater treatment, greenhouse gas monitoring, integrated biogas systems, particulate matter forecasting, data center optimization, and autonomous life cycle assessment.
