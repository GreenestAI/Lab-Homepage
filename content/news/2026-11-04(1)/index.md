Prof. SungKu Heo presented research on Process-Informed Neural Operator-based optimization of wastewater treatment plant operation at the 2026 Annual Conference of the Korean Society of Environmental Engineers (KSEE) at Jeju Shinhwa World, Jeju, Korea. The presentation focused on integrating process knowledge with neural operators for data-driven modeling and optimization of wastewater treatment plant operation.
