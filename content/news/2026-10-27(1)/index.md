Greenest A.I. Lab participated in Global Green Growth Week 2026 (GGGW 2026), hosted by the Global Green Growth Institute (GGGI) in Seoul, Korea.

The event brought together experts and stakeholders from governments, international organizations, industry, finance, and academia to discuss green growth, climate technology, sustainable development, and global cooperation.

Through participation in the event, we shared and explored recent perspectives on green transformation and climate-resilient development. Go Greenest!
