Prof. SungKu Heo was invited to present “Water-Energy-AI Nexus for Decarbonization and a Circular Economy” at the 12th International Conference on Water Resource and Environment (WRE 2026), held at the University of Seoul, Republic of Korea. The presentation introduces an integrated Water–Energy–AI Nexus framework that combines AI-driven wastewater forecasting, autonomous operation of wastewater treatment plants, and optimization of integrated biogasification systems toward decarbonization and a circular economy.
