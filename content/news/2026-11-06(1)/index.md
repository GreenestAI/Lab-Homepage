SeonJu Kim presented research on an AI-based autonomous soft-sensing system for N₂O emission quantification at the 2026 Annual Conference of the Korean Society of Environmental Engineers (KSEE) at Jeju Shinhwa World, Jeju, Korea. The presentation focused on an autonomous framework integrating sensor fault diagnosis, prediction, and real-time correction for reliable N₂O emission monitoring in wastewater treatment plants.
