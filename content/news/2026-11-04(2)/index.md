Prof. SungKu Heo presented research on AI-based inverse engineering for decentralized wastewater treatment at the 2026 Annual Conference of the Korean Society of Environmental Engineers (KSEE) at Jeju Shinhwa World, Jeju, Korea. The presentation focused on an AI-driven framework integrating influent characterization, process design, and operation for decentralized wastewater treatment systems.
